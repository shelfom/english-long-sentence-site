# Worked example · 欧洲公共预算

## 1 · 切割

61 个词，1 个句号。先在标点后回车，整句放在同一页。

```
Stretched public budgets,
squeezed between soaring debt costs and anaemic growth,
have prompted growing numbers of young people,
fearful that their prospects are fading,
to lash out in countries where cash-strapped governments,
struggling to keep their electoral promises,
defray the rising cost of entrenched entitlements by incrementally watering down the social provisions on which the most vulnerable have come to depend.
```

## 6 · 翻译

各国政府的预算本来就紧，债务开支还在猛涨，经济增长又乏力。为了兑现竞选承诺、承担越来越高的既有福利开支，一些政府只好逐步削减最缺乏保障的人赖以生活的社会支持。眼看前途越来越暗淡，越来越多的年轻人起来反抗。
